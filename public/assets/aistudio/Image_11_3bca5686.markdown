## Brand & Style

This design system powers a fast-paced, high-taste digital hub for modern culinary discovery, underground pop-ups, and dish-level micro-reviews. The aesthetic merges tactile food culture with high-precision food tech: vibrant, nocturnal, electric, and impeccably organized.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Nocturnal & Immersive:** Feels like stepping into a vibrant night market or a discreet late-night chef counter. Deep carbon and charcoal foundations let photography and live alerts radiate.
- **Electric Precision:** Neon citrus and warm fiery embers spark urgency for limited seats, drop alerts, and dish recommendations.
- **Curation with Attitude:** Confident, concise, and punchy. Micro-reviews prioritize specific dish critique over long-winded essays.

### Design Movement: Dark Tech-Bistronomy
The interface pairs the functional discipline of high-end developer platforms with the tactile warmth of contemporary food-and-wine culture. It uses crisp, subtle 1px translucent borders, localized atmospheric glows, and pill-formed interactive anchors to communicate status, availability, and curation score at a glance.

## Colors

The palette operates on rich, near-black charcoal surfaces that allow food photography and operational cues to pop with visceral clarity. 

### Color Strategy & Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#121212`):** Ground-level viewport surface, deep and quiet, reducing eye fatigue during evening browsing.
- **Surface Elevation (`#1A1A1A` to `#242424`):** Cards, flyouts, and modal panels step up in tonal luminance without shifting hue.
- **Primary Energy (`#FF5E36` / `#FF8A00`):** Electric ember and radiant persimmon. Reserved for critical call-to-actions, dish ratings, bookmarks, and drop countdowns.
- **Signal Freshness (`#06D6A0` / `#10B981`):** Hyper-fresh mint and neon lime. Dedicated strictly to real-time statuses ("Open Now", "Tables Live", "Batch Ready") and verified bite badges.
- **Border Subtle (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Micro-borders for architectural separation without visual noise.

## Typography

The type system pairs the punchy, geometric expressiveness of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for titles, badges, and dish names with the hyper-legible neutral rigor of **Inter** for micro-reviews, tags, metadata, and timestamps.

- **Scale & Performance:** Hero titles use tight letter-spacing to feel impactful and editorial.
- **Micro-Reviews:** Body text remains crisp at 14px with adequate line-height, optimized for dense discovery feeds and split-second digestion.
- **Labels:** Metric values and operational statuses leverage uppercase styling with widened tracking (`label-caps`) to stand out against high-res imagery.

## Layout & Spacing

Discovery thrives on dense, rhythmically predictable vertical stacks and frictionless horizontal carousels.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture:** 4-column layout on mobile, 8-column on tablet, and 12-column on desktop. Desktop discovery feeds cap at a maximum content width of 1280px to preserve thumbnail sharpness and reading comfort.
- **Card Clustering:** Use `space-xs` (8px) between dish tags and metadata items; use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding; use `gutter` (16px/24px) between discovery tiles.
- **Viewport Fluidity:** Micro-review cards reflow from single-column swipe carousels on handheld devices into staggered, masonry-inspired 3-column structures on wide viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical luminescence and tinted backdrops rather than conventional muddy drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Stacking
1. **Canvas Layer (`#121212`):** Base backdrop.
2. **Card Surface (`#1A1A1A`):** 1px perimeter border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to clearly define boundaries over the charcoal canvas.
3. **Elevated Floating Layer (`#242424`):** Modals, sticky filter bars, and pop-up reservation sheets. These use a delicate multi-layered ambient shadow tinted with low-alpha black: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
4. **Neon Bloom / Glow State:** Active chips, live counters, and high-rated dish highlights receive a soft radial under-glow using the primary or secondary tint: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(255, 94, 54, 0.35)` for ember and `0 0 16px -4px rgba(6, 214, 160, 0.4)` for open alerts.
5. **Glass Overlays:** Bottom sheets and floating search navigation use background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) over a 75% alpha charcoal fill.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a hyper-modern curved profile that feels fluid, welcoming, and tactile.

- **Cards & Sheet Modals:** Built with `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to soften visual density and cradle high-bleed food photography.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** Built with `rounded-md` to `rounded-lg` (8px to 16px) for ergonomic tap targets.
- **Badges, Micro-Reviews & Status Indicators:** Rendered as complete capsule/pill shapes (`rounded-full`) to immediately contrast against square card viewports and photo ratios.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book/Drop Access):** Filled gradient from `#FF5E36` to `#FF8A00` with bold text in `#FFFFFF`, subtle inner highlight, pill or 12px rounded profile. Hover transitions introduce a 20% glow expansion.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** `#1E1E1E` fill with 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, hover state lightens border to `#FF5E36` and background to `#282828`.

### Glowing Pill Tags & Status Chips
- **"Live Now" / "Pop-Up Open":** Mint background `#06D6A0` at 12% opacity, text in `#06D6A0`, accented by a pulsing 6px solid green dot and a soft mint outer blur.
- **Dish Category / Flavor Notes:** Charcoal surface `#222222`, text `#E0E0E0`, subtle light border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).

### Culinary Discovery Cards (Micro-Review & Pop-Up Tiles)
- **Container:** Rounded 24px (`rounded-2xl`), background `#1A1A1A`, 1px boundary stroke.
- **Visuals:** Top edge bleed image (16:10 aspect ratio), with gradient darkening overlay (`linear-gradient(to top, #1A1A1A 0%, transparent 40%)`) ensuring legible overlay typography.
- **Dish Score Ribbon:** Floating glass pill top right showing flame/electric badge with the micro-score (e.g., `9.6`).

### Input Fields & Filter Search
- **Search Surface:** Dark inset `#141414` with a crisp 1px stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. 
- **Focus State:** Stroke brightens instantly to `#FF5E36` alongside a faint ambient glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 94, 54, 0.2)`).
- **Text:** Placeholder text sits at muted `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`, typed input is stark `#FFFFFF`.

### Micro-Review Snippets
- Specialized layout: Mini avatar (32px pill), user handle in bold Inter 12px, single-sentence dish takeaway wrapped in high-contrast quotation, and hyper-specific metric tag (e.g., "Must Order: Charred Octopus").
